Help & frequently asked questions

Helpful information for shopping with Ovhan Global.

How do I place an order?

Browse the product catalogue, add items to your cart, then choose the WhatsApp order option. Review the message and send it to our team.

Are the products and prices confirmed?

The current catalogue contains sample listings. Contact us to confirm real stock, product details, final prices and delivery charges before paying.

Can I view prices in UGX and USD?

Yes. Use the currency selector on the product catalogue. The conversion is illustrative and must be updated to an agreed current rate before launch.

Can I pay using MTN MoMo or Airtel Money?

These payment methods are planned, but this demo does not process payments. Only pay using instructions confirmed directly by Ovhan Global through a trusted process.

Can I pay by international card?

Card payments are planned for a later stage after a suitable payment provider and secure checkout are connected.

How do I check delivery?

Include your delivery location in your WhatsApp order. Our team should confirm whether delivery is available, the cost and the estimated timing.

How do I contact customer support?

Open the menu and select Contact / inquiries, or use the WhatsApp support button.
